This zip file contains the full series of Yukon river water temparatures as collected by Al von Finster (Whitehorse, Yukon) funded by the Yukon River Panel's R&E fund.

The pdf is the final project report to the fund.
The R file is a script that merges two data files: the yukon river water temperature data series with station meta data to then allow data grouping by additional variables. 
In particular, note the variable: station_seriesName, which aggregates stations of a common river-reach when one station was decommissioned but is located close to the currently active station. The metadata also includes geographic coordinates, basin, subbasin, and subsubbasin groupings (as developed by the Water Survey of Canada)

The data loggers are still recording during 2023 but arrangements have not yet be made for their recovery and data extraction.
